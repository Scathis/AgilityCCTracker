Kits

Create kit

Recommended kits are suggested runner setups: what to wear and what to carry.

  • Runners pick one from the dropdown in the plugin's sidebar. The default kit is pre-selected for anyone who hasn't chosen.
  • Once they say they're attending as a runner, the plugin checks their gear and inventory against it until they reach the course, and outlines anything missing in their bank.
  • That check runs on their own machine; nothing about their inventory is sent back.
  • Every kit automatically includes the 150,000 coins needed to enter the course, in one inventory slot.
  • Kits are advice only. What actually gets a runner flagged is set under Settings → Runner gear rules.
